# Account, payment and subscriptions
Accepted payment: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Klarna (pay in 3) on orders over 100 EUR.
An order can be changed or cancelled free of charge while its status is "processing". Once shipped it must be returned instead.
Brightleaf Cloud (camera cloud recording): 3.99 EUR/month for 30 days of history, cancel anytime in the app; no refund for the current month.
Password reset: app > Sign in > Forgot password. The reset email is valid for 1 hour.
Invoices are attached to the shipping confirmation email and available in the account under Orders.
Business customers ordering 20+ units can request volume pricing through the sales team.
Support hours for human agents: Monday-Friday 09:00-18:00 CET. The assistant is available 24/7.

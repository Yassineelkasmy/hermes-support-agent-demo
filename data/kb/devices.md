# Bulbs, plugs, sensors, thermostat and camera
Color Bulb E27: 9 W, 806 lumens, 16 million colours, dimmable from the app only - do not use with wall dimmers, this causes flicker.
Bulb flickering: remove any wall dimmer, update firmware in the app, and check the fitting is not enclosed (overheating reduces brightness).
Smart Plug Mini: maximum load 2300 W / 10 A. Not suitable for outdoor use. Energy monitoring is available in the app under the plug's Usage tab.
Motion Sensor: CR2450 battery lasting about 2 years; the app warns at 10 percent. Detection range 7 metres, 110 degrees.
Thermostat: works with most boilers and heat pumps using 24 V or dry-contact control; not compatible with 230 V line-voltage electric heaters. Installation guide in the app; professional installation is available for 79 EUR in France, Belgium and Germany.
Indoor Camera: 1080p, local microSD recording up to 256 GB, optional cloud recording plan. Privacy shutter closes automatically when Home mode is on.
All devices work with Apple Home, Google Home and Amazon Alexa through the Hub 2 (Matter).

# Hub 2 troubleshooting
LED meanings: solid white = online; blinking white = pairing mode; blinking orange = no internet; solid red = hardware fault.
Hub offline (blinking orange): 1) check the router is online, 2) power-cycle the hub for 10 seconds, 3) make sure the hub is on a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network or wired by Ethernet - the Hub 2 does not support 5 GHz-only networks, 4) if still offline, factory reset.
Factory reset: hold the rear button for 10 seconds until the LED blinks white rapidly. This removes all paired devices and automations unless cloud backup is on (app > Settings > Backup).
Devices not pairing: keep the device within 2 metres of the hub during pairing, put the hub in pairing mode from the app (Add device), and reset the device (bulbs: switch on/off 5 times).
Solid red LED means a hardware fault: this is a warranty case, open a ticket.
The hub supports up to 100 devices. Firmware updates install automatically between 02:00 and 04:00.

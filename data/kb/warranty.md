# Warranty
All Brightleaf devices carry a 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. The Brightleaf Thermostat carries 3 years.
Warranty does not cover water damage on indoor-rated products, physical damage, or use with non-certified power adapters.
For a warranty claim we need the order number and a short description or photo of the fault. Approved claims get a free replacement shipped Express.
If the product is discontinued, we replace it with the closest current model or refund the purchase price.

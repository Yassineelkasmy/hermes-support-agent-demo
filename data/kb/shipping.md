# Shipping and delivery
Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day ship the same day; later orders ship the next business day.
Standard delivery (DPD) takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over 50 EUR, otherwise 4.90 EUR.
Express delivery (DHL Express) takes 1-2 business days and costs 9.90 EUR.
We ship to all EU countries, the UK, Switzerland and Norway. Customs duties for non-EU destinations are paid by the customer.
A tracking link is emailed when the parcel leaves our warehouse. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update.
If a parcel is more than 3 business days past its estimated delivery date, we open a carrier investigation and reship or refund once it concludes (at most 5 business days).

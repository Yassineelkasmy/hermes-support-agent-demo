# Returns and refunds
Products can be returned within 30 days of delivery, in original packaging, for a full refund.
Return shipping is free for defective or wrongly shipped items; otherwise a 4.90 EUR label fee is deducted from the refund.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the return reaches our warehouse.
Opened light bulbs and light strips that have been cut are not eligible for change-of-mind returns, but remain covered by warranty.
To start a return, the customer needs the order number; support creates a return ticket and a prepaid label is emailed within 1 business day.
Exchanges are handled as a return plus a new order.
